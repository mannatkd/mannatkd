### About Me 🌐
My interest in data analytics began during my Master’s in Dietetics at the University of Auckland, where I saw firsthand how data can drive better health outcomes. As part of my thesis at the Liggins Institute, I conducted a cohort analysis involving around 80 participants, managing and analyzing data using JMP, GraphPad Prism, and Excel. Through this experience, I developed a deep appreciation for how structured data and statistical insights can inform meaningful, evidence-based decisions.

During my clinical placements, I applied this analytical mindset in practice by delivering evidence-based nutrition advice, supporting patient behavior change, and contributing to quality improvement projects. These experiences reinforced my curiosity about data and inspired me to explore how analytics can be applied beyond healthcare.

To build on this foundation, I joined Mission Ready, where I developed hands-on skills across modern data tools and platforms. I advanced my Excel capabilities with Power Query and advanced formulas, learned to design and manage MySQL databases, and performed complex SQL queries to extract and analyze data. I also learned Python for the first time, using PySpark, Pandas, Scikit-learn, Seaborn, and Matplotlib for large-scale data manipulation, visualization, and predictive modeling. Additionally, I built interactive Power BI dashboards to transform raw datasets into meaningful, actionable insights.

Together, these experiences bridged my background in health and science with the world of data and technology, shaping my passion for using analytics to uncover insights, solve problems, and drive impactful decisions.
